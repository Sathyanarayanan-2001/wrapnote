Never start from a blank page again

WrapNote turns a few guided fields into finished minutes of meeting, formal reports and memorable study notes. It works like Word, saves every word as you type, runs offline and keeps your documents on your own devices.

Getting started

  1. Open Home.
  2. Choose New minutes (or New study notes).
  3. Type in the fields on the left. The page on the right fills in as you type.
  4. Add text, tables, pictures or screenshots with Add content blocks.
  5. Format with the ribbon, just as in Word. Choose Hide ribbon when you want more room.
  6. Choose Confirm end of document when the meeting or reading is over.
  7. Export to PDF, Word or HTML from the green banner.

Every word is saved in this browser about half a second after you stop typing. There is no Save button to remember.

Left pane: guided fields and blocks

Modes

  • Minutes & report: title, date, time from and to, location or link, host, scribe, purpose, people present and absent, agenda, decisions, action items (task, owner, due date, done) and next meeting.
  • Book & study notes: title, author, chapter or pages, date, core thesis, concepts (one "Term: definition" per line), quotes with page numbers, a mind map (indent with two spaces), active-recall questions and takeaways.
  • Blank: no fields; type straight onto the page.

Dates are typed day first (28/10/2026, or 28/10 for this year). Times are 24-hour (17:30). The week (CW44), month and financial year (FY 2026-27) are worked out from the date. A meeting from 23:30 to 00:45 is shown as 1 h 15 min, ending the next day.

The title and the purpose (or thesis) can also be edited directly on the page; the field on the left follows.

Corporate branding

In minutes mode, open Office / corporate branding to add a logo (left, centre or right), header and footer text, a page border and standard fonts.

Content blocks

  • Text & labels: type in the box; the block appears on the page as you type. Toggle bold, centre, highlight or a callout box, and add labels.
  • Images: click, drop, or select the box and press Ctrl+V. With several pictures you choose 2×2 grid, 3-column grid, side by side, or stacked.
  • Screenshots: choose extract text, insert as image, or both. Several screenshots can go in a grid, side by side, or one by one.
  • Grids fill up as you go. Choose Arrange on a picture block and pick 2×2 grid. Empty slots appear. The next picture or screenshot you add goes into the next empty slot, even if you take it later. You can also click an empty slot to add a picture straight into it. Pictures shrink to fit their cell, so nothing is cut off, and a full 2×2 grid always fits on one page. Text read from a screenshot in a grid follows the grid in its own block.
  • Reading text from screenshots: the first time, WrapNote loads its text reader (about 7 MB) from this site. After that it works offline. Pictures never leave your device.
  • Smart table: set rows, columns, style and header options before it is inserted.

The block list lets you move blocks up or down, edit, format or delete them. The page updates at once.

The ribbon

The ribbon starts in simple form: each tab's commands sit on one row. Use the arrows at the ends of the row, or swipe it, to see more. Full ribbon (top right) shows every command on two rows with group names. Hide ribbon (or Ctrl+F1) leaves only the tab names; choose any tab to bring the commands back. WrapNote remembers your choice.

Office documents and Word templates

An Office document is a brand-neutral controlled document: a classification line and a control table in the header with "Page X / Y", a cover page, revision history, a table of contents and ten numbered sections. The first page has its own footer (language, author, reviewer, source, approver and a notice); later pages show the footer notice.

  1. On Home, choose Office document.
  2. Fill in Document control on the left. Keep a [BRACKET] placeholder until the approved wording is known.
  3. Write in the sections. Headings are numbered for you (1, 1.1, 1.1.1); do not type the numbers.
  4. Choose Export, Word (.docx). Word opens it with the same styles, numbering, header, footers and fields. If Word asks to update fields, choose Yes so page numbers in the contents match Word's pages.

To switch an existing document, use Design & layout, Template. Switching to Office removes heading numbers you typed by hand, so they do not show twice.

Your own Word template: Settings, Word template, Import Word template…. WrapNote checks it on this device, removes pictures (such as logos), comments, tracked changes, labels, custom data and the names in its properties, repairs settings that break the Word file format, adds any style WrapNote needs that the template lacks, and lists what it did. Exports of office documents then use the template's styles, numbering, header and footers. Nothing is uploaded.

Source references

When you study several documents, each note can say where it came from: document name, heading, sub-heading, article or section and page. The note then shows a citation tag such as "Source: Grid Code 2023 › Frequency › Limits › Art. 4 › p. 12".

  1. In the left pane, choose Text & labels and type the note.
  2. Choose + Add source reference.
  3. Type the document name. Names you used before are suggested; pick one with a tap or with the arrow keys and Enter.
  4. Fill in what you know. Page takes one page (42) or a range (42-45). A bare number in Article / section shows as "Art. 4"; type "Clause 5.3" or "§ 2" to show it as written.
  5. Choose Done. The reference saves as you type; Undo takes it back.

For a table, picture block or ordinary paragraph, use Source in the block list, or put the cursor in the paragraph and choose References, Source reference….

The Sources page lists every note with a reference, from all your documents. Choose a document or heading, type a page (a range finds every note that overlaps it), or search the text. Tap any part of a citation to filter by it, and Open in… to go back to the note. Save as CSV saves the list you see; pages are written "p. 12" or "pp. 42–45" so a spreadsheet does not turn them into dates.

References are saved inside the document, so backups, sync, the WrapNote file and Import carry them. Exports to PDF, Word, HTML and Markdown show the citation under each note.

Finishing a document

  1. Choose Confirm end of document.
  2. Keep "Polish the text" and "Add a summary" ticked, or untick them.
  3. Choose Confirm end of document again.
  4. Export from the green banner: PDF, Word or HTML.
  5. To change anything later, choose Reopen for editing.

The polish runs on this device with fixed rules: one space between words, no space before punctuation, a capital letter at the start of each sentence, and no repeated words. It does not reword your text.

Sync between devices

  1. Open Settings on your computer.
  2. Choose Choose sync folder and pick a folder inside OneDrive.
  3. Repeat on your other computer with the same OneDrive folder.

Each session writes one compressed save file about 3 seconds after a change, and when you switch away from the tab. Each device keeps its newest 3 files. Documents are merged one by one: the newer edit wins, and deletions reach the other devices. The status at the top right always shows Saved, Saving, Changes waiting, Folder access needed or Sync problem.

Phones cannot open folders in the browser. On a phone, use Settings, Export backup and Import backup.

Import and export

  • Export a document as PDF, Word (.docx), HTML, Markdown, plain text, action-item CSV, or a WrapNote file. The Word file is a real .docx: headings, lists, tables with borders and repeating header rows, pictures, charts, equations, footnotes, comments, tracked changes, captions, the table of contents, page colour, borders, watermark, header, footer and page numbers become their Word versions.
  • Action items CSV uses UTF-8 with a byte order mark so Excel shows ₹ correctly. Columns: document, task, owner, due date (DD/MM/YYYY), status, ISO week and financial year.
  • Import action items from a CSV in the Action items field. WrapNote finds the header row even after title lines, matches columns such as "Assignee" or "Deadline", and shows every row as Ready or Skipped with the reason. Nothing is saved until you press Import.
  • Backup in Settings exports every document as one .json file. Importing shows what is new, newer, older or the same before anything changes.

Worked example

Input (all names are made up):

Date, time28/10/2026, 23:30 to 00:45
PeoplePresent: A. Rao, K. Iyer, M. Das. Absent: P. Sen
Actions"Update the plan", K. Iyer, due 04/11/2026; "Book the venue", M. Das, due 02/11/2026, done

Expected output on the page:

  • Date line: 28/10/2026 (Wed) · CW44 · FY 2026-27
  • Time line: 23:30–00:45 (1 h 15 min, ends next day)
  • Attendance: Present (3) and Absent (1)
  • Action table sorted by due date: "Book the venue" first, then "Update the plan"
  • Dashboard: 1 open action, 1 done, 0 overdue on or before 04/11/2026

Privacy

WrapNote has no account, no analytics and no server. It makes no network calls with your data. Documents are stored in this browser's IndexedDB. Sync only writes to the folder you choose. Clearing your browser's site data deletes local documents, so keep a backup or a sync folder.

Known limits

  • Text extraction (OCR) reads printed text well. Handwriting, very small text and photos taken at an angle read poorly. If the text reader cannot load (for example offline before its first use), the screenshot is kept and you type the text.
  • The final polish uses fixed rules on this device. It is not an online AI service.
  • A paragraph that does not fit at the bottom of a page moves whole to the next page. A table or picture taller than one page runs past the page edge on screen.
  • Word lays out pages itself, so a page break can fall in a slightly different place than on screen. Fonts Word does not have are replaced: IBM Plex Sans becomes Arial. Text boxes and wrapped pictures become ordinary paragraphs and pictures in line with the text. Use PDF for an exact copy.
  • Spell check uses a British English dictionary. Words only in your field can be added with Add to dictionary or in Settings, Spelling.
  • Password lock prevents accidental editing. It is not encryption.
  • Find matches text within one formatted run; a match split across bold and plain text is not found.

Troubleshooting

"Folder access needed"
The browser asks again after a restart. Select the status at the top right and allow access.
"Sync problem"
Select the status to retry. If it repeats, open Settings, Error log, and download the log. The same log is written as wrapnote-errors.log in the sync folder.
A date shows an error
Type the day first: 28/10/2026. The month must be 1 to 12, and the day must exist (29/02 only in leap years).
Pictures make the document slow
Turn on Memory saver in Settings. New pictures are resized to 1,600 pixels wide.
